# Final Whistle — Account Deletion

**Effective Date**: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}
**Last Updated**: {{LAST_UPDATED}}

This page explains how to delete your Final Whistle account and the data associated with it. You can request deletion whether or not the app is currently installed.

---

## 1. Delete in the app (recommended)

The fastest way is to delete from inside the app.

- Open the Final Whistle app or web client
- Go to the **MY** tab in the bottom navigation
- Scroll to the **Delete account** section
- Tap **Delete account**
- Confirm in the dialog

Deletion **starts immediately** and completes within 30 days at the latest.

---

## 2. Request deletion by email

If you cannot or prefer not to use the app (e.g., already uninstalled), you can request deletion by email.

- **To**: {{LEGAL_EMAIL}}
- **Subject**: `Final Whistle account deletion request`
- **Body**: Tell us the email address you used to sign up. If you used Apple or Google sign-in, please include your display name and any identifying details so we can verify ownership.

Requests are processed within **30 days**. We'll email a confirmation when the deletion is complete.

---

## 3. Scope of deletion

The following data is permanently deleted:

- Firebase Authentication account (UID, email, OAuth identifiers)
- Backend records (profile, settings, notification tokens)
- Content you authored solely yourself
- Your authoring identifiers on comments and metadata in shared clubs/tournaments (the author identifier is anonymized or removed)
- Streaks, usage statistics, push tokens

---

## 4. What we do not delete

The following data is retained for legal, operational, or shared-use reasons:

- **Shared club / tournament content** — Even clubs and tournaments you created may persist while other participants are still using them. Delete or transfer ownership before deleting your account if you want the content removed.
- **Shared scoreboard links** — Match results and standings already shared remain as content of the other participants and are not auto-removed.
- **Anonymized / aggregated usage logs** — Cannot be linked back to you and are kept for service improvement.
- **Data we are required by law to retain** — e.g., e-commerce transaction records (5 years). Stored separately and deleted at the end of the legal retention period. (We currently offer no paid features, so this generally does not apply.)

---

## 5. Processing time

- **In-app deletion**: starts immediately → completes within 30 days
- **Email request**: completed within 30 days of identity verification
- During processing, your data remains under access controls and we cease any new processing such as external sharing.
